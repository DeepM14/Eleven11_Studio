Our Story

Born From a Love of Craft

Eleven 11 began with a simple idea — beauty services should feel personal, not transactional. What started as a small styling chair has grown into a full salon and bridal studio, but the philosophy hasn't changed: listen first, then create.

Today, our team of stylists, makeup artists, and bridal specialists work together under one roof, so every client — whether it's a quick trim or a full wedding transformation — gets the same level of attention.
